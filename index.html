Who we are

A little piece of home, six thousand kilometres away.

Founded on 25 April 1991, the Guyana Canada Cultural Association (Calgary) is a volunteer-run home for Guyanese people, families, and friends across the city. We host the celebrations that keep our traditions alive, and we show up for each other in between.

Whether you arrived last month or were born here to Guyanese parents, there is a seat at the table for you.

  • Cultural celebrations that mark Mashramani, Independence, Diwali, Phagwah and more
  • Family programming that gives kids a real connection to where they come from
  • A network of neighbours for newcomers finding their footing in Calgary

The land we come from

Where the water falls for 226 metres — 741 feet — without stopping.

Kaieteur Falls drops off the Potaro River in a single, uninterrupted sheet — more than four times the height of Niagara, deep inside rainforest that most of the world has never seen. Guyana's name comes from an Indigenous word meaning land of many waters, and its people carry six ancestries under one flag.

Membership

Join the association.

Membership runs on our fiscal year, 1 May to 30 April, and keeps the association going. Choose the category that fits your household — every member gets a vote, a voice, and a second home in Calgary.

Family

$20per year

Parent or parents plus dependants living at home and/or attending an educational facility — including anyone in transition between school and work.

Individual

$10per year

One member, 18 years and over.

Senior

$5single, per year

65 years and over. A senior couple is $10 per year.

Student

$5per year

Full-time students at any secondary or post-secondary institution.

What membership gives you

  • Voting rights Vote at general meetings and elect the executive at the AGM.
  • A voice in the decision-making Bring ideas to the floor and help shape the events and direction of the association.
  • A second home A community that shows up — for the celebrations and for each other in between.

Membership categories and members' rights are set out in the association's by-laws. Not sure which category fits your household? Email gccacalgary@gmail.com and we'll help you work it out.
