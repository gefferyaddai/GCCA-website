About GCCA Calgary

Keeping Guyanese heritage alive in Calgary.

We bring Guyanese Canadians together through culture, community programs, celebrations, and meaningful connections across generations.

Who We Are

GCCA Calgary is a welcoming home for Guyanese people, families, and friends across the city.

We create opportunities to celebrate our roots, support one another, and share Guyana's vibrant traditions with the wider Calgary community.

Our Mission

To preserve Guyanese heritage, strengthen community, and create a lasting sense of belonging.

An active non-profit society, founded 25 April 1991.

How we began

It started the way most things Guyanese do — around a pot and a table.

A handful of Guyanese families in Calgary wanted their children to know Independence Day as more than a date on a calendar. So they cooked, they invited neighbours, and they made room. What started as a few households marking May 26th together became a standing invitation to anyone who calls Guyana home.

The association was founded on 25 April 1991. More than three decades on, GCCA Calgary is still a volunteer-run society with an elected executive, a year-round calendar, and a mandate that has not changed since that first gathering: keep the culture alive, and keep the door open.

  1. The first gatherings

    Guyanese families in Calgary begin meeting to mark Independence Day, Mashramani and Christmas the way they were marked back home.

  2. Founded — 25 April 1991

    Informal potlucks are formalized into the Guyana Canada Cultural Association (Calgary), with an elected executive, by-laws, and a proper calendar.

  3. Opening the doors wider

    Programming grows to include youth and sports, entertainment, and partnerships with the wider Caribbean and Calgary community.

  4. Where we are now

    Cultural celebrations, family programming, and a network that helps newcomers land on their feet in a new city.
