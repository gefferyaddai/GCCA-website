Cancellation & Refund Policy

GCCA Calgary aims to provide fair and transparent guidelines for cancellations, refunds and event changes. By registering for any event, attendees agree to the terms set out below.

Applies to all GCCA Calgary events.

If GCCA Calgary cancels an event

A full refund will be given if GCCA Calgary cancels an event due to unforeseen circumstances — weather, venue issues, safety concerns and the like.

Attendees will be notified through our website, by email and/or on social media.

If you cancel

How much is refunded depends on the kind of event and how much notice we have.

Standard events

Workshops, community gatherings and meetings.

7 or more days before
Full refund
Within 48 hours of the event
No refund

Special events

Galas, tournaments, catered events and ticketed celebrations. Because these involve pre-paid vendors and fixed costs, the windows are longer.

14 or more days before
Full refund
7–13 days before
Partial refund (50%)
Within 7 days of the event
No refund

How refunds are processed

  • Approved refunds are processed within 7–10 business days.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method.
  • GCCA Calgary is not responsible for delays caused by banks or payment processors.

Contact us

For cancellations, transfers or refund enquiries, email gccacalgary@gmail.com. Please include the event name, the name the registration was made under, and the date you registered.
